Different Browsers

  1. Google Chrome

    • Developed by Google.
    • Fast and widely used.
    • Available on Android,Windows,MacOS,IOS and more
    • Uses the Blink browser engine
  2. Mozilla Firefox

    • Developed by the Mozilla foundation.
    • Known for privacy and customiztaion.
    • Available on Windows,MacOS,Linux,Android and IOS.
    • Uses the Gecko engine.
  3. Microsoft Edge

    • Developed by Microsoft.
    • Built into windows.
    • Based on the chromium and uses Blink engine.
    • Available on Windows,MacOS,Android and IOS.
  4. Safari

    • Developed by Apple.
    • The default browser in Apple devices.
    • Known for the integration with the Apple ecosystem.
    • Uses the WebKit engine.
